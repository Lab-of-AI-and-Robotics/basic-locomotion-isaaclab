Signed roll guide
-----------------
The saved env.yaml selects:
  posture_ablation_mode: full_guide
  posture_guide_action_enabled: true
  posture_guide_observation_enabled: true
  posture_joint_target_mode: ik
  guide_roll_sign_mode: signed_lateral_accel
  guide_roll_disable_for_reverse: false
  guide_k_roll: 0.75
  guide_gravity: 9.81
  guide_roll_max: 0.523599 rad (30 degrees)
  guide_roll_rate_limit: 1.5 rad/s

The guide target is calculated as:

  a_lat_cmd = vx_cmd * wz_cmd
  roll_raw = -guide_k_roll * atan(a_lat_cmd / guide_gravity)
  roll_target = clamp(roll_raw, -guide_roll_max, +guide_roll_max)

The normal rate limiter is applied to roll_target at 50 Hz. Because
guide_roll_disable_for_reverse is false, signed vx is retained and reverse
motion reverses the guide-roll direction for the same yaw command.

Full-guide action prior
-----------------------
This policy uses the analytic IK guide as its action center. The final joint
target is:

  q_des = IK(guide_roll, guide_pitch=0, guide_height=0.34) \
          + action_scale * policy_action

where action_scale is 0.25. Do not replace the IK action center with the
default standing joint pose.
