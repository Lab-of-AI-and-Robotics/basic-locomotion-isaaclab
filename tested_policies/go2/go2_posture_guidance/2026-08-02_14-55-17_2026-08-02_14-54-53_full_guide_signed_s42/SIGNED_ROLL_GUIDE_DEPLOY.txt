Signed roll guide
-----------------
The saved env.yaml selects:
  guide_roll_sign_mode: signed_lateral_accel
  guide_k_roll: 0.75
  guide_gravity: 9.81
  guide_roll_max: 0.523599 rad (30 degrees)
  guide_roll_rate_limit: 1.5 rad/s

The training and deployment equations are:

  a_lat_cmd = vx_cmd * wz_cmd

  roll_target_raw = -guide_k_roll * atan(a_lat_cmd / guide_gravity)

  roll_target_clamped = clamp(
      roll_target_raw,
      -guide_roll_max,
      +guide_roll_max,
  )

At each policy update:

  max_delta = guide_roll_rate_limit * policy_dt
  guide_roll = previous_guide_roll + clamp(
      roll_target_clamped - previous_guide_roll,
      -max_delta,
      +max_delta,
  )

The policy runs at 50 Hz, so policy_dt is 0.02 s and max_delta is 0.03 rad
per policy update. Reverse motion reverses the required roll direction because
vx_cmd is signed.

Full-guide action prior
-----------------------
This model has posture_guide_action_enabled=true and
posture_joint_target_mode=ik. The final joint target is:

  q_des = IK(guide_roll, guide_pitch=0, guide_height=0.34) \
          + action_scale * policy_action

where action_scale is 0.25. Do not replace the IK action center with the
default standing joint pose.

Implementation
--------------
deploy/go2_posture_policy_wrapper.py reads guide_roll_sign_mode from env.yaml.
For signed_lateral_accel checkpoints it uses the equations above. Checkpoints
without guide_roll_sign_mode retain the historical deployment calculation for
backward compatibility.
